Our Process

1

Discover

We start by learning about your space, your lifestyle, and the story you want your home to tell. Through consultation and mood-boarding, we uncover your unique aesthetic.

2

Design

With your vision as our guide, we curate pieces, textures, and layouts that transform your space. Every detail is intentional, every choice artful.

3

Deliver

We bring the vision to life — sourcing, styling, and placing every element. Walk into your newly designed space and feel the difference design makes.

About EG Designs

Based in Dallas, Texas, EG Designs is a home decor studio dedicated to creating spaces that feel both elevated and lived-in. We believe your home should tell your story — through carefully curated objects, intentional styling, and designs that stand the test of time. From single-room refreshes to full home transformations, we bring artful design to everyday living.
